title: Clip and mask
sidebar_position: 6
---

`Clip` and `Mask` are children of a paint and hold one paint. `Clip` calls `clip()` and `Mask` calls `mask()` on the parent with that paint.

![Clip and mask](/img/components/masking.png)

```tsx
import {
  Canvas,
  Circle,
  Clip,
  Mask,
  MaskMethod,
  Rect,
  Scene,
} from '@thorvg/react-native-thorvg';

<Canvas width={600} height={600}>
  <Scene>
    {stripes.map((y) => (
      <Rect key={y} x={0} y={y} width={600} height={25} fill="#f472b6" />
    ))}
    <Clip>
      <Circle cx={160} cy={300} rx={130} />
    </Clip>
  </Scene>
  <Rect x={320} y={170} width={260} height={260} fill="#38bdf8">
    <Mask method={MaskMethod.InvAlpha}>
      <Circle cx={450} cy={300} rx={90} fill="#ffffff" />
    </Mask>
  </Rect>
</Canvas>
```

| Component | WebCanvas | Props |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Clip` | `clip(clipper)` | The clipping paint as the child |
| `Mask` | `mask(target, method)` | `method`: `MaskMethod`, and the masking paint as the child |

The clip or mask paint is not drawn itself.

:::note
Removing a `Clip` or a `Mask` does not reset the parent, because WebCanvas has no method to remove them. Change the `key` of the parent to draw it again without one.
:::
